We all take it easy when passing the unicorn school, right? We really don't want to harm the cute and delicate creatures!

The premise is quite simple. Use the arrow keys to steer left and right, avoiding the unicorns and most importantly... keeping the speed down!

All information you need is presented on the screen. Too busy to reflect on what goes on in the upper half? Use your ears, maybe the sound can provide some important information...

Good luck!


Much inspiration was taken from One Lone Coder's excellent videos, in particular https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KkMZI5Jbf18.
